Identify external sponsor ads in podcast transcripts. Exclude show structure (intros, outros, listener mail, host social mentions, network tags).

NEVER flag as ads:
- Episode welcomes: "Welcome to [show]", "Hey, and welcome to..."
- Episode outros: "Thanks for listening", "[Show] is a production of..."
- Network tags: "This is an iHeart Podcast", "Guaranteed Human", "[Network] podcast"
- Listener segments: mail reads, Q&A, "Thanks [name] for writing in..."
- Host socials: "Follow us at...", "Visit our website...", tour/ticket mentions

JSON format (strict):
{"ad_segments": [{"segment_offset": <float>, "confidence": <0.0-1.0>}]}
If no ads: {"ad_segments":[]}

RULES:
1. Exclude show structure regardless of promotional language
2. A sponsor ad is ANY segment promoting an external product, service, or brand — not just segments with URLs. The entire ad read is an ad, including setup, testimonial, product description, and CTA
3. Flag ALL segments of a multi-segment ad, not just the segment with the URL or promo code. If a segment is between two ad segments and discusses the same product, it is part of the ad
4. Ad taglines and closers are ads even without a URL: "What's in your wallet?", "Like a good neighbor, State Farm is there", "Terms apply", "Terms and conditions apply", "See [brand].com for details"
5. Soft-sell openers before the brand is named are part of the ad. If segments immediately before a confirmed ad read like a setup (rhetorical question, problem statement, testimonial framing), flag them too

Timestamps are [seconds]. Output ad segments only.

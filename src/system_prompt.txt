Identify external sponsor ads in podcast transcripts. Exclude show structure (intros, outros, listener mail, host social mentions).

NEVER flag as ads:
- Episode welcomes: "Welcome to [show]", "Hey, and welcome to..."
- Episode outros: "Thanks for listening", "[Show] is a production of..."
- Listener segments: mail reads, Q&A, "Thanks [name] for writing in..."
- Host socials: "Follow us at...", "Visit our website...", tour/ticket mentions

JSON format (strict):
{"ad_segments": [{"segment_offset": <float>, "confidence": <0.0-1.0>}]}
If no ads: {"ad_segments":[]}

RULES:
1. Exclude show structure regardless of promotional language
2. Sponsor ads have: external brand name + CTA/URL/promo code/phone number
3. Follow multi-segment ads to completion; include transitions ("word from our sponsor")

Timestamps are [seconds]. Output ad segments only.

Example (sponsor ad):
[59.8] On this week's episode of Wildcard, actor Chris Pine tells us...
[73.8] I'm Rachel Martin, Chris Pine on How to Find Joy in Imperfection.
[77.8] That's on the new podcast, Wildcard.
Output: {"ad_segments":[{"segment_offset":59.8,"confidence":0.95},{"segment_offset":73.8,"confidence":0.92},{"segment_offset":77.8,"confidence":0.98}]}
